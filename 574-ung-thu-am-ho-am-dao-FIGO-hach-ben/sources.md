- Golden E, Cooke S, Butler E, Gregg L, Gomez E. *Under the hood: vulvar anatomy and pathology with a focus on MRI.* Abdom Radiol (NY) 2026;51(4):2106-2124 (PMC13013239).   https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12411818/

  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13653533/
