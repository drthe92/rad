1. **Besiroglu H, Kadihasanoglu M.** The Safety and Efficacy of Targeted Alpha Therapy, Ac-225 Prostate-Specific Membrane Antigen, in Patients With Metastatic Castration-Resistant Prostate Cancer: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. *Prostate* 2025;85(6):541–557.

   - Link: https://europepmc.org/article/MED/PMC12626393

2. **Kochetova TY, Potievskiy MB, Nekrasova LA, Krylov VV, Shegai PV, Ivanov SA, Kaprin AD.** Actinium-225-PSMA versus lutetium-177-PSMA radioligand therapy for metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer: results of an observational study. *Front Oncol* 2026;16:1744007.

   - Link: https://europepmc.org/article/MED/PMC13008711

3. **Perrone E, Giordano A, Calcagni ML, Leccisotti L, Moretti R, Eismant A, Ghai K, Parkar T, Mishra A, Heidenreich A, Wirtz RM, Müller J, Greifenstein L, Baum RP.** Long-Term Safety and Survival Outcomes of [²²⁵Ac]Ac-PSMA and [²²⁵Ac]Ac-/[¹⁷⁷Lu]Lu-PSMA (TANDEM) Radioligand Therapy (PRLT) in Metastatic Castration-Resistant Prostate Cancer. *Cancers* 2025;17(3):405.

   - Link: https://europepmc.org/article/MED/PMC11816130

## Nguy cơ bằng PSMA PET/CT và định liều

4. **Widjaja L, Siegmund SC, Gildehaus FJ, Schmidt-Hegemann NS, Pomper MG, Rowe SP, Bundschuh RA, Wenter V, Sheikh GT, Klimek K, Stief CG, Zacherl MJ, Werner RA, Casuscelli J.** PET-based risk stratification for adverse events under [²²⁵Ac]Ac-PSMA radioligand therapy. *Eur J Nucl Med Mol Imaging* 2026;53(7):4360–4373.
   - DOI: 10.1007/s00259-026-07788-4 · PMCID: **PMC13197253** · Giấy phép: **CC BY 4.0**
   - Dùng cho: **26 bệnh nhân**, trung bình **3 ± 1 chu kỳ**; **38% (10/26) thiếu máu G3**; Hb giảm **p = 0,0002**; **Hb nền là yếu tố dự báo mạnh nhất** (p = 0,002); **osseous PSMA-TV** HR **3,4** (p = 0,024), **LDH** HR **7,83** (p = 0,018), **ALP** HR **3,52** (p = 0,037), **Hb nền** HR **0,16** (p = 0,0007); **31%** có biến cố thận (eGFR giảm ≥ 20%); Hình 4–5.
   - Link: https://europepmc.org/article/MED/PMC13197253

5. **Zhang H, Shi Y, Tang H, Gu H, Luo F, Ma D, Tang T, Li S.** Feasibility study of ²²⁵Ac-PSMA-CY313 dosimetry in mCRPC patients using SPECT. *EJNMMI Phys* 2025;13(1):14.

   - Link: https://europepmc.org/article/MED/PMC12858698
