# Nguồn — 525 Ung thư nội mạc & cổ tử cung: FIGO trên MRI

## Ca nguyên văn (case)
- Raja S, et al. Enhancing Preoperative Assessment of Endometrial Cancer: The Role of Diffusion-Weighted Imaging. Cureus. 2024;16(6):e62111. PMCID: PMC11238663, CC BY 4.0. (n = 31; độ chính xác xâm lấn sâu: T2WI 64,5% vs T2WI + DWI 90,3% vs T2WI + DCE 90,3%; ca 72 tuổi IA, 66 tuổi đọc nhầm nông→sâu, 51 tuổi IB.)
- Subbarayan SK, et al. Magnetic Resonance Imaging Evaluation of Carcinoma of the Cervix With Histopathological Correlation. Cureus. 2024;16(10):e71820. PMCID: PMC11570783, CC BY 4.0. (n = 45; độ nhạy xâm lấn chu cung 88,24%, lan rộng u 81,82%, hạch 56,25%.)
- Polish Society of Gynecological Oncology. Updated Guidelines for the Diagnosis and Treatment of Endometrial Carcinoma. Curr Oncol. 2025;32:340. PMCID: PMC12191832, CC BY 4.0. (Bảng đối chiếu FIGO 2023 vs 2009: IA1/IA2/IB/IC; IIA/IIB/IIC; IIIA1/IIIA2/IIIB/IIIC.)
- Artificial intelligence-based MRI for preoperative staging of endometrial cancer (meta-analysis). Front Oncol. 2025. PMCID: PMC12812754, CC BY 4.0. (AUC 0,83; 95% CI 0,80–0,86 cho xâm lấn cơ sâu.)
- Gynecologic imaging errors and pitfalls: how to avoid misdiagnosis on pelvic MRI. Insights Imaging. 2026. PMCID: PMC13586104, CC BY 4.0. (Sóng co cơ tử cung, bẫy MRI tiểu khung.)
