  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12991902/
- Swoboda M, Deeg J, Panczel M, et al. *Hydrogel-Coated Clips Are Associated with a Higher Risk of Dislocation After Ultrasound-Guided Breast Biopsy.* Diagnostics (Basel) 2026 (PMC13298152). CC BY 4.0 — n = 350; dislocation 14/20/9/38%, risk factor.
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13298152/
- Jaszewski P, Tejero H. *Core Versus Vacuum-Assisted Postbiopsy Breast Clip Migration Rates.* Cureus 2025 (PMC12166108). CC BY 4.0 — 153 ca; di trú ≥ 5 mm 11,76%; VAB 24% vs core 5,8%.
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12166108/
- Theunissen JEM, van Haaren ERM, Frotscher CNA, et al. *Combining Contrast-Enhanced Mammography and Radioactive-Free Magnetic Seed Localization of Non-palpable Breast Tumors: A Feasibility Study.* J Cancer 2024 (PMC11540500). CC BY 4.0 — magnetic seed: artifact MRI vs không artifact CEM.
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11540500/
- Rodríguez-Villena A, Schmülling UCV, Zapata IV, et al. *Implementation of a Multidisciplinary Protocol for the Safe Handling of Iodine-125 Radioactive Seeds in the Pathology Laboratory.* Int J Breast Cancer 2026 (PMC13162119). CC BY 4.0 — I-125: 4,5 mm, bán rã 59,5 ngày, ~2,8 MBq, di trú < 1%.
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13162119/
- Reinisch M, Cremer E, Pankert K, et al. *Ultrasound-Visible 3D Nickel-Titanium Clip for Tumor Localization After Neoadjuvant Therapy in Early Breast Cancer: A Prospective Multicenter Real-World Study.* Diagnostics (Basel) 2026 (PMC13564534). CC BY 4.0 — n = 324; phát hiện clip 91,1%.
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13564534/
- Galloway OW, Wilbers A, Sogade OO, et al. *A Single-Institution Comparative Analysis of Bracketed Localization for Partial Mastectomy Using Traditional Wire and Wireless Methods.* Ann Surg Oncol 2026 (PMC13452878). CC BY 4.0 — sơ đồ wire vs wireless bracketing.
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13452878/
- Kut A, Kut A, Emiroglu S, et al. *Comparison of tumor clipping and marking of skin projection methods in patients undergoing breast-conserving surgery after neoadjuvant chemotherapy: A randomized controlled trial.* Medicine (Baltimore) 2025 (PMC12419383). CC BY-NC 4.0 — RCT clip vs tattoo (mổ lại 35% vs 15%).
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12419383/
- Kang Y, Yin F, Li Y, et al. *Contrast-enhanced mammography-guided biopsy and localization: initial outcome and a single institution's experience.* Quant Imaging Med Surg 2026 (PMC13066843). CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 — sinh thiết/định vị dưới CEM (chỉ dẫn link).
